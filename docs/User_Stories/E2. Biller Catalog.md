### E2. Biller Catalog

#### US-08 See the list of billers and their required input fields (FR-7) · Must
As a customer, I want to see the available billers and what each one needs so that I can enter the right details.
- **Given** active billers (for example ElectroCo, AquaCo, TopUpNet), **when** I open the catalog, **then** I see each biller with its services and required input fields (for example meter number, phone number).
- **Given** a biller or service that is disabled, **when** I open the catalog, **then** it is not shown to me.
- **Given** a service, **when** I view it, **then** I see its minimum and maximum amount.

#### US-09 Admin adds and edits a biller and its services (FR-6) · Must
As an admin, I want to add and edit billers and services so that the catalog stays correct without changing code.
- **Given** valid details (name, service, required fields, min and max amount), **when** I add a biller, **then** it appears in the catalog and an audit record shows who added it and when.
- **Given** an existing biller, **when** I edit its fields or amount limits, **then** the change applies to new payments and an audit record is written.
- **Given** invalid details (for example a minimum above the maximum), **when** I save, **then** the change is rejected with a clear reason and nothing is saved.

#### US-10 Admin disables a biller or service (FR-6) · Must
As an admin, I want to disable a biller or service so that customers cannot pay it during a problem.
- **Given** an active biller, **when** I disable it, **then** it disappears from the customer catalog and new payments to it are rejected with a clear reason.
- **Given** a payment already in progress to that biller, **when** I disable the biller, **then** that payment continues its normal lifecycle.
- **Given** a disabled biller, **when** I enable it again, **then** it returns to the catalog and an audit record is written.
