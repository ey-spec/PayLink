### E6. Biller Integration

#### US-25 Pay an ElectroCo bill (REST/JSON) (FR-15) · Must
As a customer, I want to pay my electricity bill so that ElectroCo receives it quickly.
- **Given** a held payment for ElectroCo, **when** PayLink calls it with the payment reference, **then** ElectroCo answers within the timeout and the payment moves from SentToBiller to Completed.
- **Given** ElectroCo refuses the payment, **when** the answer arrives, **then** the payment becomes Failed and the hold is released.
- **Given** any ElectroCo call, **when** it is sent, **then** the fields and amount units are translated by the ElectroCo adapter only, and the rest of the system sees PayLink's own format.

#### US-26 Pay an AquaCo bill (XML) and handle its refusal (FR-15, §2.7 case 3) · Must
As a customer, I want to pay my water bill and be told clearly if it fails so that I know what to do next.
- **Given** a held payment for AquaCo, **when** PayLink sends the XML request and AquaCo confirms after 2 to 4 seconds, **then** the payment is Completed.
- **Given** AquaCo refuses with an error code, **when** the answer arrives, **then** the payment becomes Failed, the hold is released, and the customer gets an SMS with a clear reason mapped from that code.
- **Given** a payment above the bill amount, **when** AquaCo would reject it, **then** PayLink never sends more than the inquired bill amount.
- **Given** an unknown AquaCo error code, **when** it arrives, **then** the payment is Failed with a generic reason and the raw code is logged.

#### US-27 Accept the TopUpNet callback and ignore duplicates (FR-14, §2.7 case 6) · Must
As the system owner, I want TopUpNet's later answer handled once so that a top-up is never completed twice.
- **Given** a payment sent to TopUpNet, **when** TopUpNet replies "accepted", **then** the payment stays in SentToBiller and the customer is told it is being processed.
- **Given** a callback "success" for that reference, **when** it arrives, **then** the payment becomes Completed once.
- **Given** the same "success" callback arrives a second time, **when** it is processed, **then** nothing changes, no second charge or commission is made, and the duplicate is logged.
- **Given** a callback "failed", **when** it arrives, **then** the payment becomes Failed and the hold is released.
- **Given** a callback with an unknown reference, **when** it arrives, **then** it is rejected and logged.

#### US-28 Mark a payment Unknown when the biller times out (FR-13, §2.7 case 2) · Must
As a customer, I want to be told my payment is being processed when the biller is slow so that I don't pay twice.
- **Given** ElectroCo takes 12 seconds to answer, **when** the 10-second timeout passes, **then** the payment becomes Unknown, the customer is told it is being processed, and the money stays held.
- **Given** a payment in Unknown, **when** the late answer arrives after the timeout, **then** it is handled by the Unknown rules of US-29.
- **Given** one biller is down or slow, **when** payments to other billers arrive, **then** they are processed normally.

#### US-29 Re-query an Unknown payment after 1, 5 and 15 minutes (FR-13) · Must
As the system owner, I want Unknown payments re-checked so that they end in the right state.
- **Given** a payment in Unknown, **when** 1, 5 and 15 minutes have passed, **then** PayLink asks the biller for the status each time, using the same payment reference.
- **Given** a query answer "paid", **when** it arrives, **then** the payment becomes Completed; **given** "refused" or "not found", **then** it becomes Failed and is then Refunded.
- **Given** all 3 queries gave no answer, **when** the last one ends, **then** the payment stays Unknown with the hold kept, and waits for reconciliation.
- **Given** an Unknown payment, **when** any process runs, **then** it is never refunded automatically.

#### US-30 Add a new biller with only an integration and configuration (§2.7 case 8) · Must
As the development team, I want to add a biller without touching other modules so that growth is cheap and safe.
- **Given** a new biller, **when** it is added, **then** the change is one new adapter in the Biller Integration module plus configuration and catalog data.
- **Given** the pull request that adds it, **when** it is reviewed, **then** no file outside the Biller Integration module and configuration changed.
- **Given** the new biller is configured, **when** a payment is made, **then** it follows the normal lifecycle, fee, and notification flow without extra code.
