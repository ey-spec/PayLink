### E5. Fraud Checks

#### US-22 Reject payments above the single or daily limit with a clear reason (FR-18) · Must
As PayLink management, I want payments above the limits rejected so that accounts and money are protected.
- **Given** a bill amount above 10,000 EGP or below 5 EGP, **when** it is checked, **then** the payment is Rejected with a reason that names the single-payment limit.
- **Given** a Basic account with 4,800 EGP of bill amounts already counted today, **when** it tries a payment of 300 EGP, **then** it is Rejected with a reason that names the daily limit (5,000 EGP); **given** 200 EGP, **then** it is accepted (exactly at the limit is allowed).
- **Given** a Verified account, **when** the daily total is checked, **then** the limit is 30,000 EGP.
- **Given** the daily total is calculated, **when** payments are counted, **then** payments in FundsHeld, SentToBiller, Unknown and Completed count, and Rejected, Failed and Refunded do not (D-12).
- **Given** a payment, **when** the day is decided, **then** it belongs to the day it was created, and the day resets at 00:00 Africa/Cairo (D-13).
- **Given** the limits are checked, **when** the amounts are counted, **then** only the bill amount counts: the service fee and wallet top-ups are not counted (D-14, D-17). The wallet balance check still uses the total (bill plus fee).
- **Given** two payments from the same wallet arrive at the same moment, **when** the daily limit is checked, **then** the check and the hold happen in the same database transaction, so both cannot pass the limit together.
- **Given** a rejected payment, **when** it is Rejected, **then** no money is held and the limit values were read from configuration.

#### US-23 Block an account after more than 5 payments in 60 seconds (FR-19) · Should
As PayLink management, I want rapid repeated payments blocked so that abuse and bot activity are stopped.
- **Given** a wallet with 5 payments in the last 60 seconds, **when** a 6th is made (by the customer or by an agent on their behalf), **then** that payment is Rejected and a velocity block is placed on the customer's account for 10 minutes (D-10, D-16).
- **Given** an agent who makes more than 5 payments in 60 seconds for different customers, **when** each wallet is counted separately, **then** the agent is not blocked.
- **Given** a customer with an active velocity block, **when** the customer or an agent on their behalf tries to pay, **then** the payment is Rejected with a reason that names the temporary block.
- **Given** 10 minutes have passed, **when** the account pays again, **then** the velocity block has expired and it works normally without anyone unblocking it, unless another block is still active.
- **Given** an account with both a velocity block and a support block, **when** the velocity block expires, **then** the account stays blocked by the support block.
- **Given** exactly 5 payments in 60 seconds, **when** the account pays a 6th after the window has moved on, **then** it is accepted.

#### US-24 Support blocks and unblocks an account (FR-21) · Should
As a support employee, I want to block and unblock an account so that I can stop a suspected fraud or restore a customer.
- **Given** an active account, **when** support blocks it with a reason, **then** a support block with no expiry is created, new payments are Rejected with a clear reason, and an audit record shows who, when and why.
- **Given** a customer account with a support block, **when** an agent tries to pay on their behalf, **then** the payment is Rejected with the same reason.
- **Given** an account with one or more active blocks (support or velocity), **when** support unblocks it, **then** all active blocks are cleared, it can pay again, and an audit record lists each block that was cleared (D-10).
- **Given** a payment already in progress when the account is blocked, **when** the block is applied, **then** that payment continues its normal lifecycle.
- **Given** a user who is not support, **when** they try to block or unblock, **then** the request is rejected.
